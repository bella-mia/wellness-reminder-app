Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new reminder?
Tap the + button on the Reminders screen. Give it a name, pick an emoji and color, set your interval, and hit Save. The reminder will start sending notifications right away.
How do I turn off notifications for a specific reminder?
Toggle the reminder off using the switch on its card. You can also delete it by swiping left (iOS) or right-clicking (macOS).
My reminders aren't syncing between my iPhone and Mac. What's wrong?
DayToDay syncs via iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed into the same Apple ID and that iCloud is enabled in Settings. It can take a minute or two to sync.
How do I reset my streak?
Streaks are calculated automatically from your daily completion history. There is no manual reset — just keep completing your reminders each day to build it back up.
Does DayToDay collect any of my data?
No. All data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. See our Privacy Policy for details.
