DayToDay

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 24, 2026

All of your data stays on your own devices. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Data We Store

DayToDay stores the following information locally on your device:

DataPurpose
Reminder names, emojis, colors, and notification text Display and schedule your wellness reminders
Reminder intervals and active/inactive state Determine when to send notifications
Profile name and avatar emoji Personalize your in-app experience
Daily and all-time completion counts Show your stats and streak

This data is stored using Apple's standard UserDefaults mechanism on your device.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled, DayToDay uses Apple's NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore to sync your reminders across your Apple devices signed into the same iCloud account. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

Notifications

DayToDay schedules local notifications using Apple's notification system. Notifications are generated entirely on your device — no notification content is sent to any server. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings app.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No analytics or usage data
  • No advertising or tracking SDKs
  • No account credentials or email address
  • No data sent to any server or database
  • No data shared with third parties

Children's Privacy

DayToDay does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app requires no account creation and collects no data on our end.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
