## Overview

DayToDay is a wellness reminder app for macOS and iOS. This policy explains what data the app stores, where it goes, and how it is used. The short version: all of your data stays on your own devices. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

---

## Data We Collect and Store

DayToDay stores the following information **locally on your device**:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Reminder names, emojis, colors, and notification text | Display and schedule your wellness reminders |
| Reminder intervals and active/inactive state | Determine when to send notifications |
| Profile name and avatar emoji | Personalize your in-app experience |
| Daily and all-time completion counts | Show your stats and streak |

This data is stored using Apple's standard `UserDefaults` mechanism on your device.

---

## iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, DayToDay uses **Apple's `NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore`** to sync your reminders across your own Apple devices signed into the same iCloud account. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is subject to [Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We have no access to this data.

---

## Notifications

DayToDay schedules **local notifications** using Apple's `UNUserNotificationCenter`. Notifications are generated entirely on your device. No notification content is sent to any server.

You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings app.

---

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect any analytics or usage data
- We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs
- We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials
- We do not have any servers or databases that receive your data
- We do not share any data with third parties

---

## Children's Privacy

DayToDay does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The app requires no account creation and collects no personal data on our end.
